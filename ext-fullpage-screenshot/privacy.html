Privacy policy

Scrollwork – Full Page Screenshot (Chrome extension) · Effective 30 October 2026

Scrollwork is published by Foldwell ("we"). This policy explains what Scrollwork does with your data.

In short: Scrollwork works inside your browser. It reads a web page only when you ask for a screenshot, and your screenshots stay on your computer. Nothing about the pages you visit, and no screenshot, is sent to us or anyone else. The only outside service it talks to is Gumroad, to check your license key if you have bought Scrollwork Pro.

What Scrollwork does with the pages you capture

When you click the Scrollwork button and choose a capture, or press its keyboard shortcut, Scrollwork takes pictures of the current tab. For an entire-page capture it scrolls the page one screen at a time, briefly hides the scrollbars and elements that float over the page (such as sticky headers and cookie notices) so they don't repeat in the image, and joins the pictures into one image. Then it puts the page back as it was, at the place you were reading. All of this happens on your device.

What is stored, and where

DataWhere it's keptWhy
Your last five screenshots, with the address and title of the page each one shows The extension's own storage (IndexedDB) on your device. Older ones are deleted automatically, and you can delete all of them on the Settings page So the editor tab can show the screenshot and you can reopen it
Files you save (PNG, JPG or PDF) Your Downloads folder, or the folder you choose That is what you asked for
Your settings (download folder, image quality, whether to hide floating elements) Chrome's extension storage on your device (synced by Chrome if you use Chrome sync) To remember your preferences
The progress of a capture in progress Chrome's session storage on your device; cleared when you close the browser To show progress in the popup
A count of saved screenshots Chrome's extension storage on your device To ask for a review once, after your fifth saved screenshot
Your Scrollwork Pro license key, if you enter one, and when it was last checked Chrome's extension storage on your device To unlock Pro features, including offline for up to 7 days (see below)

Uninstalling Scrollwork removes everything it stored in your browser. Files you saved to your computer stay there.

Payments

Scrollwork Pro is sold through Gumroad, which processes the payment as the seller of record. Checkout happens on Gumroad's own web pages; Scrollwork never sees your card details. Gumroad gives you a license key, which you paste into Scrollwork. Scrollwork then sends that key, with Scrollwork's product ID, to Gumroad's license service to check that the purchase is valid and was not refunded, and checks it again about twice a day while you use Pro features. Scrollwork does not keep the email address or other purchase details that Gumroad's answer contains. Gumroad's own privacy policy covers the information you give it at checkout. You can remove the key from Scrollwork at any time on its Settings page. If you never enter a license key, Scrollwork never contacts Gumroad.

Permissions

  • Active tab: to capture the current tab, only after you click the Scrollwork button or press its shortcut.
  • Scripting: to scroll the page and hide floating elements while capturing, then put the page back.
  • Downloads: to save the screenshots you choose to save.
  • Storage: for the settings and license data in the table above.
  • api.gumroad.com: to check your license key with Gumroad, as described under Payments. Scrollwork reads nothing on this site.

What Scrollwork does not do

  • No uploading of screenshots, and no cloud storage.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or fingerprinting.
  • No collection of your browsing history.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No remotely loaded code: everything Scrollwork runs ships inside the extension.

The use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

Scrollwork is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. A change that affects how your data is handled will also be mentioned in the extension's release notes.
