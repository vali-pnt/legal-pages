The whole web page, in one screenshot

Scrollwork is a full page screenshot tool for Chrome. One click scrolls the page for you and stitches it into one tall image, top to bottom.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store

Free PNG and JPG screenshots. No account, no watermark.

A clean capture, not a pile of screens

Headers once, not on every screen

Sticky headers appear once at the top. Cookie bars and chat buttons appear once at the bottom instead of covering the content.

Web apps too

When a site scrolls an inner panel instead of the page, Scrollwork scrolls that panel and captures all of it.

Back where you were

When the capture is done, the page returns to the exact spot you were reading.

One click or one key

Use the toolbar button, or press Alt+Shift+S. The screenshot opens in its own tab, ready to save.

Mark it up (Pro)

Crop, add arrows and text, blur anything private, export a PDF, or copy it to paste anywhere.

Private

Scrollwork reads a page only when you ask for a screenshot. Your screenshots stay on your computer. Privacy policy.
