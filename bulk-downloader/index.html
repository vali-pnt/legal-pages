Download every image on a page, in one click

Gleaner is a bulk image downloader for Chrome. It finds every image, video and file on the page, even the lazy-loaded ones, and saves the ones you pick into one tidy folder.

Finds what other tools miss

Lazy-loaded images

Gleaner scrolls the page for you first, so images that only appear as you scroll are found too. Then it puts you back where you were.

Full-size, not thumbnails

It takes the largest version of responsive images, and the full-size image a thumbnail links to.

More than images

Videos, audio, CSS backgrounds, and linked PDFs, ZIPs, Office documents and more.

Filter in seconds

By type, by minimum width and height to drop icons, or by any text in the URL. Regular expressions work too.

One tidy folder

Files land in a named subfolder of Downloads, with clean names, the right extension, and nothing overwritten.

Private

Gleaner reads a page only when you click it. What it finds stays in your browser. Privacy policy.
