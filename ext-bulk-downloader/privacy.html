Privacy policy

Gleaner – Bulk Image Downloader (Chrome extension) · Effective 30 October 2026

Gleaner is published by Foldwell ("we"). This policy explains what Gleaner does with your data.

In short: Gleaner works inside your browser. It reads a web page only when you click its toolbar button, and it does not send anything about the pages you visit, or the files you find, to us or anyone else. The only outside service it talks to is ExtensionPay, to check whether you have bought Gleaner Pro.

What Gleaner does with the pages you open it on

When you click the Gleaner button, it looks through the current tab for images, videos, audio files and links to documents, and shows them to you. If auto-scroll is on, it scrolls the page to load more images and then returns you to where you were. This happens on your device. The list of files is not stored on any server and is not sent anywhere.

When you download files, your browser fetches them directly from the websites that host them, as if you had saved them yourself. Gleaner Pro's ZIP feature does the same, then packs the files into one archive on your device.

What is stored, and where

DataWhere it's keptWhy
Your settings (default folder, minimum size, auto-scroll) Chrome's extension storage on your device (synced by Chrome if you use Chrome sync) To remember your preferences
Filters and selections for an open tab Chrome's session storage on your device; cleared when you close the browser So reopening the popup keeps your choices
Whether you have bought Pro, and a count of successful downloads Chrome's extension storage on your device To unlock Pro features offline, and to ask for a review once, after your fifth download
A random ExtensionPay key, and your Pro status and email if you buy Pro Chrome's extension storage on your device, and ExtensionPay's servers To confirm your purchase (see below)

Uninstalling Gleaner removes everything it stored in your browser.

Payments

Gleaner Pro is sold through ExtensionPay, with payments processed by Stripe. When you choose to upgrade, checkout happens on their web pages; Gleaner never sees your card details. ExtensionPay stores a random identifier for your copy of Gleaner, whether you have paid, and the email address you give at checkout, so that it can confirm your purchase and let you restore it on another browser. Their own privacy policies cover how they handle that information. If you never buy Pro, ExtensionPay holds no email or payment information about you.

Permissions

  • Active tab and scripting: to read the current page, only after you click the Gleaner button.
  • Downloads: to save the files you select.
  • Storage: for the data in the table above.
  • Access to all sites (optional): requested only when you first use the Pro features "ZIP" (to fetch the files you selected from the sites that host them) or "All tabs" (to read your other open tabs). You can remove this access at any time on Gleaner's Settings page.
  • extensionpay.com: a small script runs on extensionpay.com only, to notice when a purchase is complete.

What Gleaner does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or fingerprinting.
  • No collection of your browsing history or the content of the pages you visit.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No remotely loaded code: everything Gleaner runs ships inside the extension.

The use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

Gleaner is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. A change that affects how your data is handled will also be mentioned in the extension's release notes.
